Privacy

Privacy policy, in plain words.

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

  • This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or any tracking.
  • Apart from the technical details our web host needs to serve pages, we only get personal data you choose to send us — by email, by phone, or by booking a call.
  • We never sell personal data.
  • Client data is used only to serve that client, and is deleted on request when we stop working together.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time.

Who we are

Enduent provides daily business intelligence for D2C and FMCG brands, and technology services for businesses. In this policy, “Enduent”, “we” and “us” mean Enduent, which is based in India.

This policy explains what personal data we handle through this website and when we work with you, why we handle it, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, write to aditi@enduent.com.

When you visit this website

This website doesn’t set cookies, and it doesn’t use analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking.

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel’s servers process technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • Fonts. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers, and Google receives your IP address.

When you contact us or book a call

  • Email and phone. If you write to us or call us, we receive your contact details and whatever you tell us.
  • Booking a call. Calls are booked on Calendly. Calendly collects the details you enter, such as your name and email address, and shares them with us so we can hold the call. Calendly’s own privacy policy covers its service.

We use these details to reply to you, hold the call and follow up on what you asked about. We don’t add you to a mailing list or send you marketing without asking you first.

If you use the Enduent product

  • Your business data. With your permission, we access data from the platforms you sell and advertise on — such as orders, sales, stock, listings and ad spend — through access you approve or reports you share with us. We only ever read: we never change anything in your accounts.
  • Your customers. Our product is designed not to keep your customers’ names, phone numbers or email addresses. Where customer-level data is needed, for example to measure repeat purchases, we store a hashed key instead.
  • Your team. We use the names, email addresses and WhatsApp numbers of the people you nominate to send briefs and alerts and to manage your account.
  • Plain-language briefs. To write some briefs in plain words, we may send summarised business figures — never your customers’ personal data — to an AI model provider (Anthropic). Every number in a brief is calculated by our own code.

Your data is used only to provide and improve the service for you. It’s never shared with other clients, and never sold. The detailed terms are in the agreement we sign with each client.

If you hire us for a project

For software, data and AI projects, we access only the systems and data the project needs, and use them only for your project. We’ll sign an NDA before you share anything detailed. The terms for each project are set out in its written agreement.

Who we share data with

We don’t sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run Enduent, and only so they can do that job:

  • Vercel — hosting this website
  • Google — serving the fonts on this website
  • Calendly — booking calls
  • Cloud hosting providers, including Render — running our product
  • Anthropic — writing briefs in plain language, from business figures only
  • Our email and messaging providers, including WhatsApp — delivering messages and briefs

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and call details: for as long as we need them to respond to you, and for our ordinary business records.
  • Client data: for as long as we work together. When an engagement ends, we delete your data on request, except for records the law requires us to keep.

How we protect it

  • Each client’s data is kept separate in our database using row-level security.
  • Access to client data is limited to the people who need it to run the service.
  • This website and our product use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever find a breach that affects your personal data, we’ll tell you promptly.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you, and how we use it
  • correct or update it
  • delete it
  • stop using it where you gave us consent, by withdrawing that consent
  • let someone you nominate exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to

Write to aditi@enduent.com. We may need to confirm it’s you before we act on a request.

Complaints. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, write to the same address and we’ll respond. If you’re still not satisfied, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 lets you take your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
