The expensive part

What you can't see is costing you.

These happen in food brands every week. Picture them in yours.

🗑️
Stock quietly expiring in a warehouse
Every expired pack is money you already spent — procurement, packaging, storage — going straight into the bin.
🪔
A stockout right before the festive rush
Your gift packs run out in your best city two weeks before Diwali. Those sales go to someone else.
💸
Ads spending on an out-of-stock product
Your Meta ads keep sending people to a page that says "currently unavailable" — and keep billing you for it.
⚡
A competitor's stockout you never saw
They went out of stock on Blinkit in Mumbai. Their customers searched for an alternative. It wasn't you.
📉
A search ranking slipping quietly
#4 to #9 over three weeks. By the time anyone notices, the visibility — and the sales — are gone.
⭐
Reviews repeating the same complaint
Five mentions of stale packaging in two weeks. Caught early, it's a fix. Caught late, it's a rating drop.

All of these are preventable — if you see them in time.
