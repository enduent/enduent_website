Answers, upfront.

Do I get a dashboard, or just messages?+
Both. The dashboard holds everything in one place, always up to date. The 8:30 AM WhatsApp brief makes sure the important things reach you even on your busiest days.
What does Enduent cost?+
We're in our pilot phase with five founding brands, so pilot pricing is agreed plainly on the call — a fraction of launch pricing, no surprises, no lock-in.
How long does setup take?+
Minutes of your time. You approve secure, read-only access; our team handles everything else. Your dashboard and briefs begin within the first couple of weeks.
How do you know my expiry dates?+
From the batch and inventory information your platforms and your team already maintain. We bring it together in one place and watch it for you — nothing new for you to track.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. Access is secure, read-only and authorised by you. We never modify anything in your accounts, and we never share or sell your data.

No. If you can read a WhatsApp message and approve a login, you can use Enduent. Our team does the rest.
What if I'm not on all these platforms?+
Enduent works with whatever you have. More channels make a richer picture, but two or three are enough to start.
How do you get competitor information?+
We track publicly visible information — prices, stock status and rankings any shopper can see — systematically, every day. We never access anyone's private data.
